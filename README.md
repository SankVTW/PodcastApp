This App is being made to play a downloaded podcast and a locally stored long-form music file at the same time on a phone.

I like having bed music to podcasts and most of the ones i listen to regularly don't have any. 

I making this for me but if you want to use it go ahead.

Progress will probably be intermittent and slow but the long term idea is to make it usable on a phone much like other podcast/music apps

Made in godot because its the only thing i know how to code in with any real confidence. 


Load the bed music you want and hit play then load the podcast you want then hit play.
